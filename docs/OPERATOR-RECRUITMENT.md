Status: recruitment funnel is production-ready, but no public recruitment post has been published and no calibration spend is authorized yet.

## Supply target

Recruit 30 applicants, interview 12, run eight paid calibration checks, and activate at least five operators with overlapping coverage in the initial Madhapur–Kondapur–Gachibowli and Hitech City–Jubilee Hills corridors.

Production counts only an operator in `active` status with complete paid-calibration evidence toward launch readiness. The enforced lifecycle is:

`new → shortlisted → calibration scheduled → active`

Activation requires a calibration task/receipt reference, a payout-system reference, at least the published ₹180 calibration payment, verified payout identity, and explicit passes for fresh capture, location, checklist completeness, safety/privacy, and manual review. Paused operators do not count. Rejected applicants cannot be reactivated. Five calibrated active operators is a minimum numeric threshold, not proof that any proposed store list is covered; locality and availability must still be checked before quoting delivery.

## Screening rubric

Shortlist only applicants who satisfy all required gates:

1. currently located in Hyderabad with a specific launch-zone locality;
2. reachable by phone and email;
3. can explain when and how they would safely capture public retail evidence;
4. understands that acceptance depends on the written checklist;
5. accepts the no-trespass, no-misrepresentation and stop-if-objected rules;
6. has realistic availability and transport for the stated zone; and
7. does not request an onboarding fee, credential sharing or work outside the product contract.

## Paid calibration

Do not activate an applicant based only on an interview. Schedule one controlled, paid calibration check with no customer dependency. The founder must separately authorize the calibration budget before tasks are released.

The calibration must test:

- freshness challenge compliance;
- correct location and radius;
- full shelf context plus readable close evidence;
- completion of every structured field;
- safe conduct and privacy judgment;
- response to a deliberately ambiguous observation; and
- communication when the requested evidence cannot safely be collected.

Activation requires accepted evidence under manual review. Record the observed completion time, travel distance, payout, QA time, rework requirement and reason. These observations replace planning assumptions in the unit-economics model.

## Stop rules

- Do not promise assignments or fixed weekly income.
- Do not recruit outside the launch corridors merely to inflate applicant count.
- Do not publish customer-funded work until payout reserve and locality coverage are confirmed.
- Stop a channel after 30 relevant clicks with zero qualified applications or after ten applicants yield zero calibration candidates; change the message or channel before repeating it.
