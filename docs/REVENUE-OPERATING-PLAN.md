# GroundTruth revenue operating plan

## Objective

Reach the first **$1,000 in monthly recurring revenue** through repeat retail-verification work. Marketplace listings, test payments, automated acknowledgements, and unpaid evaluations do not count.

## Commercial wedge

GroundTruth verifies retail execution for emerging consumer brands:

- Is the SKU actually available?
- What price is displayed?
- Is the agreed promotion or display live?
- Is the product visible, damaged, or expired?

The buyer receives fresh evidence, structured observations, a dashboard, and CSV/API-ready results. Blockchain, MCP, and x402 are integration options—not the lead sales message.

## Launch market

Operate initially in **two compact Hyderabad zones**, unless the founder can personally supervise the first 50 visits in another city. Do not advertise citywide coverage until the operating thresholds below are met.

## Offers

| Offer | Price | Included |
|---|---:|---|
| Launch pilot | $199 once | 25 accepted checks in one agreed zone, one repeatable checklist, fresh evidence, dashboard and export |
| Monitor | $249/month | 25 recurring checks, dashboard and CSV/API-ready export |
| Operations | $599/month | 75 recurring checks, scheduled batches and priority QA |
| Additional standard check | $8 | One accepted check inside the agreed zone |
| Rush check | From $12 | Only after a worker accepts; never guaranteed before coverage confirmation |

Travel outside the agreed zone, purchases, entry fees, rush work, and specialized inspections are quoted separately. The $0.01 and $0.10 tiers are integration tests. A $2 field check is a subsidized evaluation, not sustainable commercial pricing.

Two Operations subscriptions generate $1,198 MRR. Four Monitor subscriptions generate $996 MRR before additional checks.

## First buyer profile

An emerging food, beverage, personal-care, or household brand that:

1. sells through 30–300+ offline outlets;
2. is expanding into new retailers or cities;
3. cannot independently see current shelf execution;
4. has a reachable founder, sales head, or trade-marketing lead; and
5. can select 25 locations and one objective retail question.

Priority accounts: Rage Coffee, Open Secret, Beyond Snack, Beco, and MasterChow. Every message must reference a public expansion or distribution fact and one concrete audit hypothesis.

## Unit economics guardrail

Planning assumptions only; replace with observed pilot data.

- Worker pay: ₹180 minimum accepted short check within a 3 km home zone
- Average travel allowance: ₹45
- QA/support: ₹55
- Retry/fraud reserve: ₹35
- Payment/infrastructure: ₹20
- Estimated COGS: ₹335 per accepted check
- Standard price: approximately ₹704 / $8
- Estimated contribution: approximately ₹369 / 52%

Never lower worker pay to preserve an advertised price. Change scope, cluster density, or buyer price instead.

## Thirty-day execution

### Week 1 — supply and sales readiness

- Define two operating zones and one checklist: open status, displayed price, and shelf availability for up to three SKUs.
- Recruit 30 operator applicants; interview 12; run eight paid field tests; activate five.
- Complete 15 calibration checks with 100% manual QA.
- Build 30 qualified brand accounts and send ten deeply personalized founder-led messages per day.

### Week 2 — first paid pilot

- Close one prepaid 25-location pilot.
- Release work in clustered batches.
- Manually review every submission.
- Track completion time, acceptance, rework, cost per accepted check, and discrepancies found.

### Week 3 — repeatability

- Activate eight reliable operators.
- Deliver the pilot as a discrepancy-first report, not a photo archive.
- Run a results review with the buyer and propose Monitor or Operations.

### Week 4 — recurring revenue

- Close the first monthly subscription.
- Close or schedule the second paid pilot.
- Publish only verified operating metrics and an anonymized case study with buyer permission.

## Operating thresholds

Do not expand beyond the two zones until GroundTruth achieves:

- 50 consecutive accepted checks;
- at least 90% first-pass acceptance;
- less than 10% reassignment;
- positive contribution margin;
- a repeat order from an external paying buyer.

## Metrics that count

- qualified business conversations;
- paid pilots;
- accepted paid checks;
- cost per accepted check;
- gross margin;
- pilot-to-subscription conversion;
- monthly recurring revenue;
- customer repeat rate.

Do not present test transactions, demo campaigns, marketplace sales counters, or self-generated tasks as revenue traction.

## Kill rule

After 30 qualified accounts and two completed paid pilots, continue this wedge only if at least one buyer repeats or gives a concrete, fixable reason for not repeating. Otherwise change the vertical or offer; do not hide weak demand behind more features.
