# GroundTruth field-operator SOP

## Initial task scope

A standard check covers one public retail location and up to three SKUs. The operator records:

1. storefront/open status;
2. shelf availability;
3. displayed price;
4. promotion or display status when requested; and
5. two to four fresh photographs that satisfy the task challenge.

Do not enter staff-only areas, purchase restricted goods, confront store staff, record private conversations, or intentionally capture faces, payment cards, identity documents, children, or private addresses.

## Eligibility

An operator must have:

- a smartphone capable of fresh in-app capture and location reporting;
- reliable access to an assigned launch zone;
- local-language fluency;
- a verified payout identity and payment destination;
- completion of a paid calibration task; and
- acceptance of evidence, safety, privacy, and dispute rules.

GroundTruth never charges an onboarding fee.

## Compensation

- ₹180 minimum for an accepted short check within the operator's 3 km home zone
- ₹12 per additional kilometre when approved before acceptance
- ₹60 rush premium only for an accepted sub-two-hour assignment
- ₹250–₹350 for a pre-scoped 30–45 minute detailed audit
- mandatory purchase or venue-entry cost reimbursed separately only when pre-authorized

Pay weekly. Disclose acceptance criteria and compensation before the operator claims the work. Do not withhold undisputed pay because of an unrelated rating.

## Capture procedure

1. Read the full brief and acceptance criteria before travelling.
2. Confirm the public location and personal safety.
3. Start the capture session only after arriving.
4. Capture the required wide storefront/context image.
5. Capture shelf, price, SKU, promotion, or display evidence exactly as requested.
6. Include the per-task freshness challenge using the requested angle, object, or challenge card.
7. Enter structured answers from what is visible; use `not observable` instead of guessing.
8. Review every image for legibility and unnecessary personal data before submission.
9. Leave immediately if staff asks the operator to stop or conditions feel unsafe.

## Quality controls

- fresh in-app capture; gallery uploads are not accepted;
- server time and device clock-skew checks;
- location samples before and during capture;
- randomized freshness challenge;
- perceptual-hash duplicate and replay screening;
- OCR/SKU/signage consistency checks;
- route and travel plausibility checks;
- manual QA for every operator's first 20 jobs;
- risk-based review after qualification;
- a 10% blind re-check sample during the launch phase.

Evidence is not marketed as unforgeable. Device attestation can be added later, but day-one trust comes from layered capture controls, human QA, re-checks, and honest confidence limits.

## Acceptance and redo

An accepted check must satisfy every required evidence field. A redo is requested when evidence is missing, stale, illegible, outside the allowed radius, inconsistent with the structured answers, or unnecessarily captures sensitive information. A scope change from the buyer is a new task, not an unpaid redo.

## Incident escalation

Stop and notify the operator lead for:

- staff conflict or a request to leave;
- unsafe travel or location conditions;
- a task involving private property or sensitive people/data;
- suspected fraud, coercion, or duplicate evidence;
- a mandatory purchase or fee not included in the brief; or
- any request that conflicts with the acceptable-use policy.
