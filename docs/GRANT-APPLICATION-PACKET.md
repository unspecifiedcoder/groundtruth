### One-line description

GroundTruth is an API-native field-evidence network that lets software and autonomous agents pay for fresh, structured verification of present physical-world conditions.

### What are you building?

GroundTruth closes the gap between digital automation and current physical reality. A buyer defines an objective field check—such as whether a product is actually on a shelf, its displayed price, or whether a promotion is correctly executed. GroundTruth dispatches a local operator, collects fresh photographic and structured evidence against a predefined checklist, reviews the submission, and returns an auditable result through a dashboard or API.

The live product includes an asynchronous task API, MCP and A2A interfaces, a machine-readable service catalog, a field-operator workflow, evidence review, and HTTP 402 payment support. GroundTruth currently exposes canonical Base USDC as a payment rail for agent-initiated evaluation and integration flows. Small $0.01 and $0.10 tiers test payment and orchestration plumbing only; they are not represented as fulfilled fieldwork. Commercial field campaigns are separately scoped and coverage-confirmed.

The initial commercial wedge is retail execution verification for emerging consumer brands: availability, displayed price, promotion, facings, and shelf placement. The longer-term infrastructure opportunity is a permissioned physical-verification layer that any software workflow or autonomous agent can call before acting on a stale physical assumption.

### Problem

Retail systems, web search, distributor reports, and agent memory describe digital records or historical state. They do not prove what is physically true at a location now. Brands therefore make merchandising, replenishment, and expansion decisions using incomplete or stale outlet-level data. Autonomous workflows face the same boundary: they can search and transact, but they cannot directly observe a shelf, storefront, package, asset, or site.

### Why now?

Agentic software can increasingly initiate transactions and workflows without a human manually coordinating every step. That makes reliable paid tool calls important, but it also exposes a hard boundary: many economically relevant conditions exist only in the physical world. GroundTruth combines a human execution network with structured machine-readable evidence so those workflows can purchase a narrowly scoped observation rather than pretend a digital source proves current physical state.

### Why Base?

Base is GroundTruth's preferred onchain payment environment because it combines native USDC liquidity, low-cost EVM settlement, a growing agent ecosystem, and x402-compatible payment infrastructure. GroundTruth already advertises canonical Base USDC through its production HTTP 402 service manifest. The next Base milestones are to harden wallet-native settlement, add a first-class Coinbase developer integration where it materially reduces buyer friction, and measure external paid usage rather than internal test transactions.

### Business model

- Fixed-price launch pilot: $199 for 25 accepted retail checks in one confirmed operating zone.
- Recurring plans: $249 per month for 25 accepted checks and $599 per month for 75 accepted checks.
- Future API usage: per-check pricing based on operator payout, review cost, travel/coverage constraints, and GroundTruth's platform margin.

No revenue or repeat-customer claim should be made until independently verified payment and fulfillment records exist.

### Current stage and evidence

- Live production application and developer interface.
- Live HTTP 402 service challenge with Base USDC support.
- Working mission creation, operator submission, evidence review, and machine-readable result paths.
- Public pilot offer and buyer scope workflow.
- Public operator application flow and internal review pipeline.
- No verified external revenue, no completed paid commercial pilot, and no repeat customer yet.
- Marketplace counters, test transactions, registry listings, and automated acknowledgements are not treated as traction.

### Near-term milestones

Funding would be used to reach verifiable commercial and operating milestones rather than inflate top-of-funnel activity:

1. Recruit and vet an initial operator pool in compact Hyderabad zones.
2. Complete 8 paid calibration checks and retain at least 5 operators who meet the acceptance standard.
3. Deliver at least 50 accepted commercial checks and publish completion time, first-pass acceptance rate, exception rate, review cost, and payout cost.
4. Convert 2 brands into paid $199 pilots.
5. Convert at least 2 buyers into recurring plans and exceed $1,000 MRR.
6. Harden Base USDC settlement, reconciliation, and buyer receipts for production use.

### Use of funds

- Field-operator recruitment, training, calibration, and payouts.
- Campaign pre-funding and payment reconciliation controls.
- Evidence-quality and fraud-review tooling.
- Base USDC and wallet integration hardening.
- Two tightly scoped design-partner pilots.
- Legal, privacy, and operational controls for physical fieldwork.

### Defensibility

GroundTruth's intended moat is not a generic task marketplace or a photo upload endpoint. It is the accumulated operating system for translating machine requests into objective field protocols: coverage data, operator reliability, evidence templates, acceptance rules, exception handling, buyer-specific schemas, and repeatable fulfillment economics. These advantages must be proven through paid campaigns; they are not yet claimed as established.

## Fetch.ai / ASI application decision

### Apply only if this commitment is real

GroundTruth should apply to the Fetch.ai Startup Accelerator or submit an ASI partnership/funding request only if the founder is willing to implement and maintain a real integration with the Fetch/ASI stack. The credible proposal is:

> Build a Fetch-compatible physical-verification agent that accepts a structured request from another agent, dispatches an asynchronous GroundTruth field mission, returns structured evidence and status events, and exposes transparent payment and fulfillment state.

Do not describe the current MCP/A2A implementation as a Fetch/uAgents integration. It is not one yet.

### Additional Fetch application inputs required

- Founder name, email, phone, city, LinkedIn, education, and employment history.
- Whether the founder is full-time and whether any employment or exclusivity constraints exist.
- Founder introduction video.
- Legal company details, cap table/equity split, and team composition.
- Verified traction and revenue metrics; zero is acceptable and must be stated honestly.
- Prior funding and the requested funding amount/use.
- A concrete Fetch/uAgents/Agentverse technical milestone the founder is prepared to deliver.

### ASI/CUDOS path

The ASI partnership form currently includes a grant or funding request category, while the CUDOS accelerator offers compute credits and onboarding. Use the ASI partnership form for a concrete ecosystem-integration proposal. Use CUDOS only if GroundTruth can quantify a real compute workload; field-operator payouts and customer acquisition are not compute-credit use cases.
